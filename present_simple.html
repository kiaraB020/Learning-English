Present Simple

Learn how and when to use the Present Simple tense in English.

When we use the Present Simple

1. General facts that are always true and permanent:

  • Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius.

  • The sun rises in the east.

2. Habits and Routines:

  • I usually wake up at 7 am.

  • She always drinks coffee at breakfast.

3. Permanent Situations (States or jobs):

  • He teaches history at the local high school.

  • She works at Google.

Structure

REMEMBER: When we use HE, SHE or IT, we add -s to the verb.
